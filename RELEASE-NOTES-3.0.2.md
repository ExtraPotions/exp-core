# exp-core 3.0.2

Shared matte toggle chrome for ExtraPotions product menus: theme-surface 34 × 20 tracks, 14 × 14 rounded-square knobs, High Contrast and forced-colors, and Pride ON states that stay matte instead of rainbow fills. Products vendor this Core output during their build and keep local fallbacks.

Existing V3 settings are preserved. Approved artwork is unchanged.
Diagnostics stay local; page text, form values, URL queries, and unrelated storage are excluded by the shared diagnostics helper.
