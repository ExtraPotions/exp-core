# ExtraPotions menu guide

[Back to Core](../README.md)

## Open a product

Install a product, not Core. Each product bundles the shared menu. Each product has a tab on the right edge of the window; on Twitch and Kick each product has its own icon in the top bar instead. Products keep their own features, colors and settings.

The launcher uses Twitch’s and Kick’s top bars where available. Elsewhere, Reddit included, the tabs sit on the right edge and can be dragged up and down together; Shift+drag changes their order. A tab shows a dot when its product has an update. Where a top bar has room for only one icon, the products share one launcher there.

## Menu size and navigation

Choose Standard, Compact or Mini in **Look → Menu Size**. Product sections are drawers: one opens at a time, the menu reopens on the last one used, and groups inside a drawer also open one at a time. Header buttons open the changelog, donations and Close. Support and Reset stay inside **System**.

## Status, help and recovery

**System** shows Working, Waiting, Paused or Needs Attention and offers a recovery action when available. Use Show or Copy beside Diagnostics for troubleshooting, Report a Problem for support, and Reset All Settings only when you want to clear the product’s settings.

SHIFT and PRISMA are retired; no other product requires them.
